Foundation

Pre-Launch

Prepare the marketing materials, landing page and lead-capture journey before any paid campaign starts.

For: A project with approved information that is preparing to go to market.

Timeline: set in your proposal · Review rounds: 2

Includes

  • Project brief, audience, value proposition and core messages — built from your approved information
  • Sales collateral: digital brochure (about 12 pages), fact sheet (about 2 pages), sales presentation (about 10 slides) and buyer FAQ
  • Launch content: 6 ad and social designs, 3 ad copy variations and 2 short-video scripts from your existing visuals
  • One responsive project landing page with inquiry form, consent notice and confirmation
  • Lead routing to your agreed inbox or supported CRM, with tracking and test submissions
  • Launch plan: channels, audiences, budget allocation, schedule, reporting plan and readiness checklist

Complete when Collateral is delivered, claims are approved by you, the landing page and form are tested, lead routing and tracking are verified, and the launch plan is handed over.

What we need from you

Approved project information, plans and visuals, one named approver, and the inbox or CRM that will receive inquiries.

Campaign activation

Launch

Activate the agreed digital campaigns using the Pre-Launch infrastructure — or equivalent assets you already have.

For: A project whose materials and landing page are ready, and that needs campaigns live now.

Initial monitoring: typically 14 days · Media budget paid by you

Includes

  • Campaign and ad-set structure for the agreed platforms
  • Audience and targeting configuration, with approved ad copy and assets
  • Tracking, landing page and lead-routing checks before going live
  • Campaign activation, initial monitoring and troubleshooting
  • Initial performance report for the launch period (typically 14 days, or as agreed) with next-step recommendations

Complete when Campaigns are live as agreed, tracking and lead routing are verified, and the initial report is delivered. Setup and initial monitoring are separate from ongoing monthly management.

What we need from you

Approved materials and a working landing page (ours or yours), ad accounts owned by you with access for us, and the agreed media budget.

Optimization and growth

Growth Launch

Improve active campaigns and the inquiry journey through monthly analysis, controlled testing and optimization — also available for campaigns you already run.

For: A project with live campaigns that needs more, and better, inquiries.

Billing: monthly · Report: monthly

Includes

  • Monitoring and management of the agreed active campaigns
  • Controlled tests of audiences, messages or creative variations
  • Landing page review and limited conversion improvements
  • Review of inquiry volume and the lead-quality data you share
  • Monthly performance report with priorities for the next period
  • Review meeting or written review

Each month Channels, campaigns, tests, reports and meetings included are defined in your proposal.

What we need from you

Access to the active ad accounts and analytics, and the lead-quality feedback your sales team shares each month.

FAQ

Clear answers before you ask for an assessment.

Can I buy one stage only?

Yes. If your materials and landing page are already ready you can start at Launch, and if campaigns are already running you can start at Growth Launch. Pre-Launch can also be bought on its own.

Do you guarantee leads or sales?

No. We commit to the agreed deliverables, setup, tracking and reporting. Lead volume, cost per lead and sales depend on the project, market, offer, budget and your sales follow-up, so we do not guarantee them.

Who pays for the advertising?

You do — directly to the advertising platforms, from accounts you own. Our fees cover the work, not the media budget.

Do you produce renders, films or photography?

Not within these stages. We use your approved assets. If you need new visual production, it can be commissioned separately through Graphics House or other suppliers.

How is pricing set?

Through a tailored written quotation after a short project assessment, based on scope, deliverables, languages, channels, integrations and your readiness — not a one-size-fits-all price.

How long does each stage take?

It depends on scope and on how quickly materials and approvals arrive. Launch includes an initial monitoring period of typically 14 days, and Growth Launch runs monthly. Your proposal sets the exact timeline.

Who is responsible for advertising licenses?

You are, as the developer. Real estate advertising and off-plan sales in Saudi Arabia require valid licenses and approvals; we include your license details wherever the platforms or regulations require them.

Who owns the ad accounts and the materials?

Advertising accounts should be owned by you, with access granted to us. Delivered materials and configurations are handed over to you as agreed in the proposal.

Our materials are not ready yet. Can you still help?

Yes. Pre-Launch organizes and prepares marketing materials from your approved information. If you also need new renders, films or photography, they can be arranged separately — usually through Graphics House.
