What does Bees Motion do?

We are a digital marketing company: strategy, social media, paid ads, landing pages and reporting — with in-house creative production for the content.

No. Those are sectors where we have documented results. We work with any business that wants to grow online.

We commit to the agreed work, tracking and reporting. Sales depend on your offer, budget and follow-up, so we don't promise numbers we can't control.

Who pays for the ads?

You do, directly to the platforms and from accounts you own. Our fee covers the work.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the services, channels and content volume. After a short call we send a written proposal.
