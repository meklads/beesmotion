FAQ

Clear answers before we start.

What does Bees Motion do?

We build lead-generation systems in three stages — Pre-Launch, Launch and Growth Launch. Content, social media, ads and landing pages are included where each stage needs them.

Do you offer social media management on its own?

Not as a standalone service. Social media and content are part of the system, tied to a clear goal: qualified leads.

Do you only work with real estate and healthcare?

No. They are the sectors with our documented results. The system works for any business launching or growing an offer.

Do you guarantee leads or sales?

We commit to the agreed work, tracking and reporting. Results depend on the offer, budget and your sales follow-up, so we don't promise numbers we can't control.

Who pays for the ads?

You do, directly to the platforms and from accounts you own. Our fee covers the work.

How is pricing set?

After a short assessment we send a written proposal for the stage you need, with scope, deliverables and timeline.
