The challenge

The reality of patient experience today

Patient confusion

Unclear wayfinding creates stress and wastes time for patients and families.

No structured journey

Patients do not know what to expect at each step, which raises anxiety.

No journey data

Hospitals lack visibility into movement paths and friction points.

Operational drag

Staff spend too much time guiding and answering questions that can be automated.
