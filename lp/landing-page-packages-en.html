FAQ

Before you decide

Why not send the ad to my main website?

A main website offers many choices that distract the visitor. A landing page shows one thing and asks for one action, so it is easier to measure and improve.

Where do enquiries go?

To your inbox or a supported CRM that we agree on, and we test sending before launch.

Do you write the copy, or do I send it?

We write it in Arabic and English. You send us the offer details and any photos or video you have.

Where is the page published?

We agree on where it is published with you before we start.

How long does it take, and how many revisions?

It depends on the package. The timing and revision rounds are written into your proposal before you commit to anything.

Do you guarantee a number of enquiries?

No. We do not guarantee enquiry volume or cost, which depend on the offer, the price and the ads. We guarantee that the page, form and tracking work as agreed.
