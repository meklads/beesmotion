FAQ

Before you decide

What do I need to send you?

Your logo, any photos, videos or designs you have, and a short description of your business and audience. If you have no material, we arrange a shoot or produce from your project designs.

How many reels do I get, and how many revisions?

It depends on the package. The reel count and revision rounds are written into your proposal before you commit to anything.

When do I receive my first reels?

Production starts once you approve the ideas and scripts, and the delivery date is written into the proposal.

Do you post the reels for me?

We deliver ready files with post copy and a suggested schedule. If you want us to handle posting, we agree that as part of the package.

Do you film on site?

Yes, when needed, and it is set within the package. We can also produce complete reels without a shoot, from your material or project designs.

Do you guarantee views or enquiries?

No. We guarantee the quality of the work and delivery on the agreed date. Results depend on the market, the offer and paid promotion.

Is there a long contract?

No. Packages run month to month.
