The eight angles

Lifestyle

FAQ

Before you decide

Who is this for?

Real estate developers, offices and brokers, marketing teams at property companies, and agencies.

What do I send you?

Property photos, renders, floor plans, project details, any existing video, and the project's brand identity if you have one.

We have no renders. Can we still start?

Yes. We start from the photos you have, or our group produces renders from your project plans under a separate proposal.

Do you add rooms or features that do not exist?

No. We show only what is in your file, and we label conceptual scenes as conceptual.

How many reels do I get, and when?

It depends on the package and the size of the project. The count, delivery date and revision rounds are written into your proposal before you commit to anything.

Do you run the ads as well?

Yes, in the Content + Activation package. In the other two, we deliver ready content to your team or your agency.

Do you guarantee enquiries or sales?

No. We guarantee the quality and accuracy of the content and delivery on the agreed date. Results depend on the market, the price, the offer and the ad budget.
