Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 September 2026

Bees Motion (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through beesmotion.com, how we use it, and how you can contact us.

Information we collect

When you use our contact form or reach us on WhatsApp, we may collect your name, company, phone number, email, industry, project need, objective, and message. We may also receive basic technical data such as browser type and pages visited through standard website hosting and analytics tools.

How we use information

We use your information to respond to enquiries, prepare proposals, deliver services you request, improve our website, and communicate about projects. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

We may share information with sister companies within Tasami Group (including Graphics House and Turriva) when a project needs their specialized delivery, and with service providers who host or operate systems on our behalf, under appropriate confidentiality. We may also disclose information when required by law.

Retention

We keep enquiry and project-related information for as long as needed to handle your request, deliver work, meet legal obligations, or resolve disputes, then delete or anonymize it when no longer required.

Your choices

You may ask to access, update, or delete personal information we hold about you, or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, by contacting us using the details below.
