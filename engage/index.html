Investment policy

Investment is set after a short discovery and a written scope. We do not publish a fixed price menu — every brief differs in objective, channels, and production depth.

Primary next step: a 15–20 minute discovery call. Detailed proposals follow alignment — not a long form first.

Investment process

How scope and investment are set

This is how premium, brief-led work is priced: understand the goal, define the scope in writing, then confirm investment before kickoff.

  1. 01

    Discovery call

    A short call to clarify objective, audience, timeline, and which path fits (Foundation, Growth, or Campaign).

  2. 02

    Written scope

    Deliverables, duration, and responsibilities — clear enough to approve before any production starts.

  3. 03

    Proposal & kickoff

    Investment confirmed against the scope. Work starts only after alignment — no surprise invoices for undefined work.
