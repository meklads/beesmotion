FAQ

Questions decision makers ask before booking

What does the MedMotion™ system include?

Content strategy, motion and cartoon series, visual identity for episodes, publishing calendar, and engagement measurement in one system.

Must every series be produced at once?

Not necessarily. We help prioritize by brand stage and budget. Real value appears when everything shares one visual language.

We already have a social team. Do we still need this?

Often yes. The team moves the message. MedMotion™ prepares the content product itself, the visual language, and persuasion assets. Many posts underperform because the visual product is not ready.

Does it work for hospitals outside Jeddah?

Yes. We serve medical institutions across Saudi Arabia, the GCC, and Europe, with remote production and platform-ready delivery.

How long does the first series take?

It depends on scope. We set a clear timeline from idea to delivery, with early samples before full production.
